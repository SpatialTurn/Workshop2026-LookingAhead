Designing and building your own geospatial teaching module as a Carpentries-style lesson (Markdown basics, creating a lesson repository from the Workbench template, working with GitHub Desktop, and adding episodes), with additional readings on data curation, FAIR data, metadata and data sharing.

This module was taught as part of the Purdue-NSF GIS Cybertraining for the Social Sciences Workshop, a two-day workshop held at Purdue University in 2026 (Day 2, Putting it all Together, for all participants). See the [Workshop 2026 page](https://spatialturn.github.io/workshop2026.html) for the full program.

It is part of [Enabling the Geospatial Turn in the Social Sciences](https://spatialturn.github.io/), a set of training modules developed at Purdue University with support from the National Science Foundation.
